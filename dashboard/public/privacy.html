Raya Attendance – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

The Raya Attendance app and web dashboard record when Raya employees start and finish work at their assigned workplace. This policy explains what information is processed, why, how long it is kept and what your rights are. The data controller is Raya, your employer. The app is only for Raya employees; it cannot be used without an account created by Raya's HR department.

1. Information we process

We do not collect contacts, photos, messages, other apps' data, advertising identifiers or your location outside check-in/check-out. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.

2. Who can see it

  • You: your own attendance history in the app.
  • Your manager: attendance of their own team only.
  • Raya HR and system administrators: everyone's attendance, to run payroll, review unusual check-ins and manage accounts.

We do not sell or rent personal information and do not share it with third parties, except service providers who host the system for Raya (Google Cloud) and Google Play's integrity service described above, or when the law requires it.

3. Where and how it is stored

The data is stored on a server operated for Raya in Google Cloud's Johannesburg (South Africa) region. All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as secure one-way hashes. Records of attendance and security events cannot be altered, and every change made by staff is recorded in a tamper-evident audit log.

5. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable laws you may ask to access the information held about you, ask for corrections, object to processing, or complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Because attendance records are needed for your employment, some requests (such as deleting attendance records) may be limited by law.

6. Permissions the app asks for

  • Location – only while the app is open and you check in or out.
  • Camera – only to scan an office QR code, if your workplace uses one.
  • Internet – to send the check-in to Raya's server.

You can withdraw these permissions in your phone's settings at any time; you will then be unable to check in with the app, and HR can record your attendance another way.
